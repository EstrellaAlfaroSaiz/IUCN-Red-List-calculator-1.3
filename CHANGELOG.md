# Changelog

## 1.3.0

- Preview and filter GBIF occurrences, and combine them with your own point layers.
- Calculate Area of Occupancy (AOO) and Extent of Occurrence (EOO), and preview editable distribution polygons.
- Export Point Distribution as SRedList-compatible CSV or as a shapefile, export selected layers as GeoJSON, and request an official GBIF download DOI.
- Connects distribution patches by default, dissolves overlaps in separate mode, and aligns final point and polygon fields and citation guides with the IUCN spatial attribute standard.
